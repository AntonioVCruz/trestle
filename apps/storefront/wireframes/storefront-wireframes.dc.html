Flow

Would skip shipping for digital-only carts. Blocked: ADR 0016 requires shipping on every cart, and the catalog has no "requires shipping" flag yet.
Going back • Review shows every section with "Edit" → jumps to that step. • After editing, "Continue" moves forward through later steps that depend on the change, then returns to Review. • Changing the shipping address makes the API drop the shipping method.
